## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-octane, retro-futuristic arcade aesthetic rooted in 1980s synthwave, outrun car culture, and cabinet coin-op nostalgia. It is tailored for high-energy mobile racing and stunt gameplay, invoking sensations of midnight velocity, CRT raster monitors, and electric neon boulevards.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Retro-Futurism / Synthwave Arcade:** The interface fuses hyper-saturated neon glows with midnight abyss backdrops. Key visual motifs include isometric wireframe perspective grids, chrome specular highlights, scanline overlays, and dynamic speed-slanted panels.
- **Tactile Cabinet Hardware:** Buttons evoke mechanical coin-op chassis with layered, gradient-beveled borders and inner light strikes. Interactive states pulse with electric energy rather than standard flat fills.
- **Emotional Target:** Adrenaline, speed, nostalgia, and arcade precision.

## Colors
The palette is built strictly for dark-mode display, creating sharp visual punch through intense luminescent accents over deep midnight purple and void-like navy.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#FF007F` - Hot Magenta):** Critical driver interactions, nitro meters, primary CTA buttons, record-breaking callouts, and outer neon glow signatures.
- **Secondary (`#00F0FF` - Electric Cyan):** Stunt multipliers, vehicle tech readouts, shields, secondary CTAs, and digital speedometers.
- **Tertiary (`#FFE600` - Sunset Neon Yellow):** Coin balances, high-score alerts, hazard zones, and turbo overdrive indicators. Can shift toward fiery orange (`#FF7700`) in gradients.
- **Neutral Surface Base (`#0D0221` / `#120526` - Midnight Void):** Deep chromatic canvas tones that eliminate screen glare while making neon glow layers pop.

### Color Application Rules
- **No pure neutral grays:** All structural fills must pull from the midnight purple spectrum (`#1A0B36`, `#25144A`).
- **Gradients:** Use directional linear angles (typically 135deg or 180deg) blending `#FF007F` to `#FF7700` (Sunset Nitro) or `#00F0FF` to `#7928CA` (Hyperdrive).

## Typography
Typography channels arcade velocity, pairing the heavy, aggressive architecture of Syne for headers with the clean tech-forward legibility of Space Grotesk and monospaced telemetry via JetBrains Mono.

### Hierarchy & Style Treatments
- **Headers (Syne):** Displayed in uppercase. For primary tournament screens and race win titles, apply a 6° forward skew (`transform: skewX(-6deg)`) to reinforce kinetic momentum.
- **Data & Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** Numbers, lap timers, drift points, and tachometer feeds utilize monospace fonts to prevent layout jittering during high-speed number ticking.
- **Text Shadows:** Headlines feature subtle double-drop neon text glows (`0 0 8px rgba(255, 0, 127, 0.6), 0 0 20px rgba(255, 0, 127, 0.3)`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system accounts for touch-first thumb zones across portrait garage/loadout menus and landscape in-race HUD telemetry.

### Screen Adaptation & Grids
- **Portrait Views (Upgrades, Gacha, Leaderboards):** Structured on a flexible 4-column layout on mobile, expanding to 8 columns on tablet. Margins are kept compact (`1rem` on mobile) to preserve maximum screen real estate for 3D car hero views.
- **Landscape Views (Race HUD):** Anchored by perimeter safe zones. Critical inputs (Steer, Nitro, E-Brake) cluster in the bottom 25% corner arcs, while HUD elements (Mini-map, Speedometer, Stunt Chain) float along the top and upper lateral margins.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a strict 4px base multiplier, favoring dense, arcade-style packing for telemetry modules and generous air around 3D vehicle canvas viewports.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is rendered through luminous radiance, chromatic stacking, and glassmorphic translucency rather than daylight drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Depth Model
- **Level 0 (Backdrop Void):** Solid dark navy-purple (`#0D0221`) layered with an infinite perspective wireframe grid moving toward a vanishing horizon line. A subtle CRT raster scanline CSS pattern overlays this level.
- **Level 1 (Sub-Panels & Data HUDs):** Deep glassmorphism. Background fills use `rgba(26, 11, 54, 0.65)` with a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a 1px border of `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Overlays):** Richer purple containers with vivid neon perimeter borders (`#FF007F` or `#00F0FF`) generating diffuse ambient box glows (`0 0 16px rgba(255, 0, 127, 0.35)`).
- **Level 3 (Modal Alerts & Interactive Nodes):** Metallic chrome highlights on top edges (`linear-gradient(180deg, #FFFFFF 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0) 100%)`) with dual-color drop shadows (cyan floor reflection + magenta top rim).

## Shapes
The shape grammar embodies late-80s arcade hardware: sharp, aerodynamic, and industrial. Pure pill shapes and large bubbly radii are prohibited.

### Geometry Details
- **Base Geometry:** Standard elements use compact, crisp corners (`0.25rem` / `4px`).
- **Chamfered / Angled Edges:** High-priority cards, modal dialogs, and primary action buttons utilize a `clip-path` 45° corner cut (chamfer of 8px to 12px) on alternating diagonal corners (top-right and bottom-left) to simulate armored chassis panels.
- **Slanted Containers:** Section badges and speed indicators employ an italicized parallel-polygon silhouette (`skewX(-10deg)`).

## Components

### Buttons & Arcade Triggers
- **Primary Action (Start Race / Nitro):** Chamfered rectangular profile with a hot magenta-to-orange gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FF007F, #FF7700)`). Top edge carries a 1px white specular chrome line. The button casts an outer glow of `0 0 20px rgba(255, 0, 127, 0.6)`. Text is uppercase Syne Bold, slanted -6°.
- **Secondary (Tuning / Upgrades):** Dark purple glass surface bordered by a 1.5px neon cyan stroke (`#00F0FF`). On press, shifts to solid cyan with contrasting `#0D0221` dark text.
- **In-Race HUD Buttons:** Circular touch triggers with thick gradient border rings, semi-transparent dark center (`rgba(13, 2, 33, 0.5)`), and centered glowing vector glyphs.

### Cards & Vehicle Pods
- Translucent midnight backing (`rgba(22, 7, 46, 0.75)`), 1px gradient stroke running from neon pink to deep indigo.
- Header bars within cards have a horizontal scanline texture and monospaced telemetry metadata tags.
- Outer perimeter carries a soft neon under-glow.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Compact, angular badges with a 2px diagonal cut on the top-right corner.
- Inactive: Bordered by muted purple-blue (`#2D1B4E`), body text in gray-cyan (`#7EE7F2`).
- Active: Filled with electric cyan (`#00F0FF`), black label font (`#0D0221`), with a distinct cyan bloom effect.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- Dark inset wells with an inner shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.8)`) evoking recessed cabinet coin slots.
- Active state lights up a 1px electric magenta border and flashes a blocky neon cursor.

### Checkboxes & Toggles
- **Checkboxes:** Diamond or angled-square bounding boxes. Checked state displays an electric cyan laser crosshair checkmark.
- **Toggles:** Beveled track resembling a slider switch on an arcade console; thumb knob is a faceted neon pill that pulses yellow-orange when enabled.

### Progress & Boost Gauges
- Segmented LED meters rather than continuous smooth bars. Segments light up sequentially from neon cyan through sunset yellow to danger magenta at peak overload.
